About the Artist

I'm Azan — the person behind AZART.

AZART is more than a portfolio to me. It's a record of how I'm learning, experimenting, and developing my voice through digital art.

A little about me

I’m Azan, a digital artist from Pakistan with a passion for character art, portraits, illustration, and visual experimentation. I started creating because I enjoyed the process of taking something that existed only in my imagination and giving it a form I could actually see.

I'm still learning, still experimenting, and constantly finding new things I want to improve. That's one of the things I enjoy most about art — there is always another technique to explore and another idea to try.

I don't want every artwork to look the same.

I'm interested in discovering what different colors, compositions, lighting styles, and techniques can do to a piece. Some experiments work exactly as planned. Others teach me something completely unexpected.

How I approach art

I usually start with the idea rather than the technique. Once I know what I want the artwork to communicate, I build around the character, composition, lighting, and color until everything begins to feel connected.

Skills

Digital Art

Creating Finished digital pieces through drawing, rendering, lighting, color, and composition.

Character Art

Exploring character design, expressions, poses, clothing, and atmosphere.

Portraits

Focusing on facial structure, expression, lighting, and personality.

Tools

Most of my artwork is created digitally, using the tools that let me focus on drawing rather than getting in the way of the process.

Sketchbook

My primary tool for creating and developing most of my digital artwork.

Still becoming a better artist

My artistic journey is ongoing. Every finished pieces gives me something to learn from, and every new project is an opportunity to push a little further than the last one.
