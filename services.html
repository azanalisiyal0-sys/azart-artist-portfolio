Discuss a Project

From idea to final artwork

01 - Brief

You tell me what you're looking for, share your references and requiments, and we establish what the final artwork should achieve.

02 - Sketch

I turn the idea into an initial composition, focusing on the pose, proportions, framing, and overall direction.

03 - Refinement

Once the foundation works, I develop the details, colors, lighting, and atmosphere of the piece.

04 - Final Artwork

The artwork is polished, reviewed, and prepared in the appropriate format for the agreed use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I provide references?

Absolutely. References are often helpful for understanding the character, style, mood, composition, or direction you're looking for.

Can I Request Changes

Yes. The process includes opportunities to review the direction before the final artwork is completed. The exact number of revisions can be discussed depending on the project.

Do you accept commercial projects?

Yes, depending on the project. Commercial usage, licensing, and other requirements should be discussed before the artwork begins.

How much does a commission cost?

Pricing depends on the type of the artwork, complexity, size, intended use, and deadline. Contact me with your project detail for a quote.
